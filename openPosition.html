Open Positions

Opportunities to join the Medical Innovation Research in AI (MIRAI) Laboratory at Üsküdar University, Türkiye.

MSc Positions

If you are already an MSc student at Üsküdar University, and you would like to either do your Graduation Project with me or join the MIRAI Laboratory, then please email me your CV and a brief description of your research interests. Current MSc thesis topics supervised in my group include real-time EEG-based mindfulness state detection, EEG biomarker detection using machine learning, and AI-based educational technology adoption in health sciences.

If you are not at Üsküdar yet and are seeking funded MSc positions, I currently do not have dedicated funding for MSc applicants. However, you are welcome to apply directly to the university's Software Engineering programme and reach out to me afterwards.

How to apply: Send your CV and a short description of your research interests to faezeh.rohani@uskudar.edu.tr.

PhD Positions

While I am always looking for self-motivated PhD students with a strong background in artificial intelligence, EEG/ERP signal processing, or biomedical data analysis, I recruit doctoral students primarily through graduate programmes at Üsküdar University. Prospective students should apply directly to the relevant graduate programme and email me after admission.

I am particularly interested in candidates with backgrounds in machine learning, neuroscience, biomedical engineering, motor behavior, or related fields. Active research directions in my group include: intelligent diagnostics for ADHD and other neurodevelopmental disorders, explainable AI for clinical decision support, EEG/ERP-based brain-computer interfaces, gait and motor performance analysis, and AI-driven mental health informatics. I also collaborate with the Brain and Trauma Foundation Grison (Switzerland) and the Pitt HexAI Research Laboratory (University of Pittsburgh) on interdisciplinary AI and healthcare projects.

Interested applicants are encouraged to review the MIRAI Laboratory page to learn more about current research activities and ongoing projects.

Note: Please apply first to the graduate programme at Üsküdar University, then email me at faezeh.rohani@uskudar.edu.tr with your admission confirmation and a brief research statement.

Postdocs and Visiting Scholars

If you are interested in joining my research group as a postdoctoral researcher or visiting scholar, please email me your CV, a letter outlining your research interests, and — for visiting scholar positions — information about your proposed visit dates and funding sources. Collaborative areas of particular interest include EEG/ERP signal processing, machine learning for mental health diagnostics, explainable AI in healthcare, motor behavior analysis, and neurofeedback-based interventions.

I receive many emails regularly. If you do not hear back within two weeks, it likely means I am not able to move forward at this time. I sincerely apologise for any delay. All CVs and materials you send will be kept strictly private.

Interested applicants are also welcome to explore the MIRAI Laboratory page to learn more about our research activities and international collaborations.

Please note: Postdoctoral positions are subject to funding availability. Visiting scholar positions are welcome year-round — please include your proposed visit dates and funding plan in your initial email.

Letters of Recommendation for Graduate School Applications

I am generally happy to write letters of support for graduate school applications, scholarships and fellowships, research programmes, internships, and positions in academia or industry — for students and researchers I have worked with directly, whether at Üsküdar University, Sanabad Golbahar Institute, or through collaborative research projects.

To request a letter, please email me at least four weeks before the deadline with the following: the name and description of the programme, the deadline, your CV, your personal statement (if available), and a brief note reminding me of our collaboration and why you feel I am a suitable recommender.

Please understand that I may decline if I feel I cannot write a sufficiently strong letter, or if the timeline does not allow adequate preparation. In such cases, I will let you know as early as possible so you can seek alternative recommenders.

Get in Touch

For any inquiries about positions or collaborations, feel free to reach out directly. I look forward to hearing from motivated researchers who share a passion for advancing AI-driven solutions in healthcare, neuroscience, and mental health — particularly in EEG signal processing, machine learning, neurofeedback, and intelligent diagnostics.
