Teaching

Teaching has been a core part of my professional life for over 14 years. My courses range from foundational programming and data structures to advanced topics in machine learning, prompt engineering, large language models, and AI applications in healthcare. I design my classes to connect theoretical principles with practical, real-world problems — drawing from my own research in brain disorder diagnosis, EEG signal processing, and intelligent health systems to keep the material grounded and relevant.
