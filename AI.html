AI Consulting Services

My work sits at the crossroads of artificial intelligence, neuroscience, and clinical health. As an Assistant Professor with a PhD in AI and Robotics and a neurofeedback practitioner since 2015, I bring hands-on expertise in machine learning, EEG and ERP signal processing, brain disorder diagnosis, and intelligent healthcare system design. My research has addressed conditions such as ADHD, anxiety, PTSD, and depression, combining biosignal data with advanced computational models to support clinical decision-making.

I provide AI consulting for organizations and researchers working on healthcare technology, neurotechnology, and AI-powered diagnostic or therapeutic systems. This includes building and validating machine learning pipelines for biomedical data, designing neurofeedback and cognitive intervention frameworks, integrating AI into clinical workflows, and supporting research projects from concept through publication. I collaborate with the Brain and Trauma Foundation Grison (Switzerland) and the Pitt HexAI Research Laboratory (University of Pittsburgh), bringing an international research perspective to every engagement.

The first 30-minute consultation is free of charge. After that initial session, fees are agreed upon based on the nature and scope of the project. To get in touch, send an email to faezeh.rohani@uskudar.edu.tr with the subject line “AI Consulting Services”.
